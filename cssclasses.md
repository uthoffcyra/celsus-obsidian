# cssclasses

Here's a list of custom css classes in my [main CSS snippet](https://github.com/uthoffcyra/celsus-obsidian/blob/main/snippets/celsus.css).

### hide-title

Hides the built in H1 element. Used for notes with headers different than their file name.

<img width="2870" height="1884" alt="20251105 14 24 58@2x" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9f2e74e3-4a5a-4e0f-b4ca-a82db3c96bc2" />

### boxlinks

Turns links into full-width buttons.

<img width="2870" height="1884" alt="20251105 14 26 24@2x" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/82fa4285-112d-44ba-ab57-d5db5ef5fa6e" />

### full-width

Allows the note content to take up the full width of the window.

<img width="2870" height="1884" alt="20251105 14 27 45@2x" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d654c14-4192-4e1f-bb6d-65d331be58c1" />

### colorless

Removes color from italicized and bold elements.

<img width="2870" height="1884" alt="20251105 14 28 53@2x" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/41d969fc-0780-48f7-ba75-b2a197c7a472" />

### justify

Applies 'justify' alignment to text.

<img width="2870" height="1884" alt="20251105 14 30 04@2x" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c48dae7a-4a1f-45a1-aaae-95b65517432c" />

Makes the heading larger and centered, and changes the color of the checkboxes. Used for my daily notes.
